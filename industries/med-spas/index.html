What a good med spa ad looks like

Most people think about a treatment for a while before they book. The ad's job is to make the first step feel easy.

A consultation offer

A free or low cost consultation is an easy yes. It gets someone in the door to talk with your provider before committing to anything.

One treatment per campaign

Injectables, laser hair removal, facials and body treatments attract different people. Each gets its own ad, message and page.

People close enough to come back

We target by distance from your location and by interest in beauty and skincare, so the budget reaches people who can become regulars.

Your real space and team

Photos and short videos of your rooms and providers do better than stock images. We help you plan what to film.

Careful claims, clear information

Aesthetic treatments are medical services, and the ad platforms have rules about how they can be advertised. We write with that in mind.

  • No promised results in any ad or page
  • Plain descriptions of the treatment and who performs it
  • Offers stated clearly, with any conditions spelled out
  • You review and approve everything before it runs

Follow-up that turns interest into a booking

Someone who fills out a form at night is easy to lose by morning. We set up the follow-up so they hear from you while they are still thinking about it.

A landing page for each treatment

What it is, what a first visit looks like, and a short form or booking link.

A reply within minutes

A text and an email go out right away with a link to choose a consultation time.

Every lead in one place

Your team sees who asked about what, who booked and who still needs a call.
