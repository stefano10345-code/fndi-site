What a CRM does for you

A CRM is a shared list of every lead and customer, with notes on where each one stands.

One list of leads

Names, phone numbers, messages and notes are kept together, not spread across inboxes and notebooks.

A clear pipeline

See who is new, who has been contacted, who booked, and who became a customer.

Reminders to follow up

Each lead has a next step and a date, so follow-up does not depend on memory.

Why it matters for your ads

Ads bring leads in. Without a system, some of them sit in an inbox until they lose interest. A CRM also shows which ads produced actual customers, which helps us spend your budget better.

  • Each lead is tagged with the ad or page it came from
  • Automatic texts and emails can start from the CRM
