What we automate

Simple messages, sent at the right moment, written in your words.

Instant reply to new leads

When someone fills out a form, they get a text or email right away with the next step.

Follow-up messages

If a lead does not answer, a short series of messages checks in over the following days.

Appointment reminders

Booked customers get a reminder before their appointment, which helps reduce no-shows.

Alerts for your team

You get a notification when a new lead comes in, so you can reach out while they are still interested.

Review requests

After a visit, customers get a message asking them to leave a review.

Check-ins with past customers

Past customers get a message inviting them to come back.

Why it matters for your ads

You pay for every lead an ad brings in. If nobody answers for a day, that person has often moved on. Automatic follow-up makes sure each lead gets a reply, even when you are busy with customers.
