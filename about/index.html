About FNDI

We are a small Miami agency that helps local businesses get more customers. Most of that work is Facebook and Instagram advertising.

Book a Free Call See How It Works

Our Story

Stefano Fernandini and Douglas O'Keefe met in middle school in Miami. They always knew they would build something together.

They kept seeing good local businesses struggle to bring in new customers, while bigger companies had whole marketing teams and budgets to match. So they started FNDI to do that work for the businesses around them.

FNDI stands for Future Native Digital Intelligence.

Where it started

Two friends from Miami who saw local owners doing great work and not getting found.

March 2026

Stefano and Douglas launch FNDI.

Today

We run Meta ads for local businesses and build the websites, CRM and follow-up that turn those ads into customers.
